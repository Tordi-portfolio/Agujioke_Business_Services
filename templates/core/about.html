Who we are

{{ SITE.name }} is a Lagos-based business services company led by {{ SITE.owner }}. We operate across trade, construction, travel, immigration, real estate and regulated product categories, which lets us offer something unusual: a single partner who can support a project from the first idea to final delivery.

Our founder works as a self-employed entrepreneur and consultant, helping foreign companies operate in Nigeria and Africa. That mission shapes everything we do. International firms often arrive with excellent products, capital and expertise, but without a local team that understands the registrations, relationships and realities of the market. We fill that gap.

We also support Nigerians and Africans who want to trade internationally, expand their businesses, invest in property, travel abroad or formalise their companies. The same principles apply whether you are a multinational or an individual: careful preparation, honest communication and respect for the rules.

What we do

Our work falls into eleven connected service lines: import services, export services, general merchant supply, general contracting, travel and tours, immigration consultancy, real estate, FMCG distribution, business registration, wines and spirits and pharmaceutical supply. Each service has its own page with a detailed explanation of what we offer and how the process works.

What makes the combination powerful is that the services connect. A foreign investor might register a company with us, rent an office through our real-estate service, bring staff in with our immigration support, source equipment through our import service and have the premises fitted out by our contracting team. A Nigerian exporter might use our business registration, export and travel services to build and meet an international customer base.

How we work

We begin by listening. Most problems in trade and project delivery start with unclear requirements, so we ask detailed questions before offering any plan. Then we give you a written quotation, explain the steps and set realistic expectations on time and cost.

During delivery we coordinate the many parties involved, such as suppliers, shipping lines, clearing agents, government agencies, lawyers, surveyors, contractors and transporters, and we keep you informed. Where regulation matters, as it does for food, drugs, alcohol, immigration and property, we are open about what is required and we work strictly within the law.
